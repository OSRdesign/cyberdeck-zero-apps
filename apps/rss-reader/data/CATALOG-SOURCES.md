# Catalog provenance

`catalog.json` (269 feeds, generated 2026-10-18) is a hand-curated list written by the project from the publishers' own
official feed URLs and re-verified with curl on that date (fields `items`, `newest`, `checked`). No third-party feed list was
copied wholesale. Feed URLs are facts; feed content belongs to the publishers and is only fetched and shown on the device,
never mirrored or re-hosted.

Public lists surveyed while building it (selection only, for cross-checking and future expansion):

* plenaryapp/awesome-rss-feeds (CC0-1.0), tuan3w/awesome-tech-rss (CC0-1.0), Alplox/awesome-chilean-rss (CC0-1.0): free to reuse.
* kagisearch/kite-public and kagisearch/smallweb (MIT), clivoa/awesome-security-feeds (MIT): reuse with the MIT notice kept; the
  preferred sources if the catalog is expanded.
* Lists with no licence, a copyleft licence (GPL, AGPL) or proprietary terms (Feedly, Inoreader, Feedbin) were not used.

Any expansion must re-verify every URL (reachable, parses, `items` and `newest` recorded) before it ships.
