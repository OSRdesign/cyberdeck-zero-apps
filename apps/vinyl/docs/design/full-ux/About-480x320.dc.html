Vinyl 0.1.0
A music player for the CyberDeck Zero boards. MIT licence.
FORMATS
MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV with small decoders; AAC, ALAC, WMA and the rest through FFmpeg 7.1.
THIS OUTPUT
Sharkoon Mobile DAC: -45.0 to 0.0 dB, 23 levels of 2.05 dB.
LIBRARIES
libmpg123, libvorbisfile, libFLAC, libopus, libpulse, FFmpeg libraries, LVGL.
